# Plan the day / what to do next

## Inputs (all in your context or the brain)
Today's brief (overdue, due today, this week), open `tickets/` (status ≠ resolved),
"Open threads" in recent digests, `inbox.md` size, calendar *(when connected)*,
and what the user says about time and energy right now.

## Ranking
1. P1 tickets, security, anything blocking several people.
2. Overdue tasks, then tasks due today.
3. Promised to someone (callback, mail, quote), especially clients waiting on the user.
4. Quick wins (< 15 min) when the user has little time or energy.
5. Due this week, high priority (⏫).
Inbox with more than ~10 items: suggest 10 minutes of sorting.

## Output
- **Now:** one task + why (one line) + the first concrete step.
- **Then:** 2 more max.
- **Can wait:** one line naming what you deliberately left out.
- Offer one shortcut ("Shall I draft the mail to X?").
If the user asks for a full day plan: time blocks, with the hard tasks in the morning
and calls/mails grouped, leaving buffer for incoming support.
Write the plan into `journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (append) if the user accepts it.
